How it works — all in-browser, no server needed

Everything runs inside your browser — no downloads, no server, no CORS:

  • 1Parse markers — Transcript split at every [screenshot:N] tag into segments.
  • 2TTS timing — SpeechSynthesis speaks each segment silently (volume 0). Duration measured on onend.
  • 3Record audio — SpeechSynthesis speaks again at volume 1. Audio is captured via getUserMedia and encoded to AAC using WebCodecs AudioEncoder.
  • 4Encode video — Screenshots encoded as H.264 frames via WebCodecs VideoEncoder at 10 fps.
  • 5Mux MP4 — Pure-JS ISO BMFF muxer writes a single MP4 with both video (H.264) and audio (AAC) tracks — fully seekable.

Audio capture uses getUserMedia (microphone permission). On most laptops the mic picks up the TTS playing through speakers. For best results use headphones and speak in a quiet environment — or use a loopback device.

Browser: Chrome 110+ or Edge 110+ recommended — uses AudioContext.setSinkId({type:'none'}) for completely silent generation. Chrome 94–109 will ask for microphone access as a fallback.
Audio: On Chrome 110+, generation is fully silent — no speakers, no microphone needed. On older Chrome, TTS plays through speakers briefly while the mic captures it.
