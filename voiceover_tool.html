How it works — processing pipeline

The backend (generate_voiceover.py --serve) performs these steps for each [screenshot:N] segment:

  • 1Parse markers — Transcript is split at every [screenshot:N] tag into narration segments.
  • 2TTS per segment — gTTS generates a separate MP3 for each segment's narration text. Duration is measured exactly.
  • 3Video per segment — FFmpeg loops the referenced screenshot image for exactly that segment's audio duration.
  • 4Concatenate video — All per-segment video clips are joined in order.
  • 5Concatenate audio — All per-segment MP3s are joined in order.
  • 6Mux — Combined silent video + combined audio are merged into the final MP4 (-c:a aac).

Result: each screenshot is visible for exactly as long as its narration takes to speak — no manual timing needed.

Marker rules & tips
RuleDetail
Syntax[screenshot:N] where N is 1-based (matches upload order)
PlacementStart of a paragraph, inline mid-sentence, or on its own line
ReuseReference the same screenshot multiple times — it stays on screen for the combined duration
No markersIf there are no markers, all text is narrated over screenshot 1
Missing imageIf you reference [screenshot:5] but only uploaded 3 images, generation will fail with a clear error
Other brackets[pause], [note: ...] etc. are stripped from audio but do not affect screenshot switching
